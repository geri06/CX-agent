haddock Customer Support Knowledge Base

Welcome to the haddock Knowledge Base. This comprehensive guide is designed to empower restaurant managers and administrators to optimize margins, control supplier costs, and streamline back-of-house operations with ease. haddock is designed to learn as you go, making your management more automated every day.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------


1. Getting Started & Account Configuration

User Settings and Preferences

To begin, access your workspace configuration by clicking the blue restaurant/user icon in the upper-left corner of the interface.

* Language Selection: Select your preferred interface language (Spanish, English, or Portuguese) from the settings menu.
* Dashboard Preferences: Navigate to Settings > Dashboard to customize your view. You can toggle between viewing data inclusive or exclusive of VAT, and choose to display sales based on the "base" amount or the "total" amount.
* Tax Defaults: For Spanish operations, you can enable the "10% automatic VAT" toggle. This ensures your sales calculations remain consistent with standard hospitality rates.

Managing Users and Roles

Collaboration is key to success. You can invite team members and assign specific permissions via User Settings > My Restaurants > My Team.

Role	Permissions & Access
Administrator	Full access to all functionalities: dashboards, products, documents, and settings.
Document Management	Can add, view, and edit documents and upload sales. No access to the dashboard or financial totals.
Manager View	Read-only access to the dashboard, supplier data, and exports. Cannot edit information.
Chef	Exclusive access to Bill of Materials (scandallos) and Products. No access to the dashboard or documents.

Adding a Team Member:

1. Navigate to User Settings > My Restaurants.
2. Select the specific restaurant and go to My Team.
3. Click Invite Member (or New User).
4. Enter the team member's Email and select their Role.
5. Click Send Invitation. (Note: You can add up to 7 users depending on your plan).

To delete or edit a member, select the user in the My Team section and use the three dots menu to modify their role or remove access.

Subscription and Billing

* Plans: The Premium plan is our most popular choice, supporting up to 400 documents per month and granting full access to the Product Module for price variation control.
* Managing Limits: Upgrade your plan at any time via User Settings > My Restaurants > Change Plan. Please note that deleted documents still count toward your monthly digitization limit.
* Payment & Invoices: We support Credit Card and Direct Debit (Direct Debit is available for annual subscriptions; contact support to activate). Download your invoices by clicking the Download Icon in your billing history.

2. Document Digitization & Management

The Upload Workflow

haddock makes digitizing expenses simple with three flexible methods:

1. Mobile/Photo: Capture documents instantly using the haddock mobile app.
2. PDF/File Upload: Drag and drop files (PDF or JPG) directly from your computer via New Expense > Search File.
3. Email: Forward digital invoices to your unique haddock email address for automatic processing.

Pro Tip: In the beginning, you may manually select the category (e.g., Raw Materials) and document type. However, haddock learns as you go—after the first few uploads, the system will recognize your suppliers and automate this data entry for you.

Document Processing States

* Digitized: Ready for your review.
* Under Review: Currently being processed by our algorithm. If you realize an upload was a mistake, you can click the three dots to Delete it even while it is under review.
* Lacking Confirmation: Requires your attention. Common causes include Poor image quality, Missing supplier NIF/VAT, or missing legally required data like totals or dates.
* Rejected: The algorithm has flagged an error. This usually happens with Mixed documents (multiple invoices in one photo) or Duplicates.

Editing and Manual Entry

* Manual Entry: If a physical document is missing, use "Add data yourself" to record the expense.
* Editing Digitized Data: Open any document and click the three dots to Edit. Use the pencil icon next to line items to adjust quantities, prices, or VAT.

Advanced Filtering and Exporting

Find what you need in seconds using filters for date range, supplier, category, or payment status.

* How to Export: Select your documents and click Export.
* Formats: Available in Excel, PDF, or CSV.
* Breakdown Options: Choose from Summaries (totals), By Products (individual line items), or VAT in Columns (accounting view).

3. Supplier and Product Management

Supplier Profiles

Create new profiles via Suppliers > New Supplier.

* Non-Editable Security: Once created, the NIF/CIF, Fiscal Name, and Address cannot be changed to ensure tax compliance.
* Real-Time Data Toggle: Enable "Deliver with delivery notes/invoices for tickets" for suppliers who provide delivery notes. This ensures your dashboard reflects current spending before the final invoice arrives.

The Product Module & Price Alerts

haddock tracks every ingredient belonging to Raw Materials, Beverages, or Cleaning.

* Price Variation: A red arrow indicates a price hike, while a green arrow shows a decrease.
* Reference Price: By default, this is the Weighted Average Price. However, if you set a Tariff Price (your negotiated rate), it overrides the average as the benchmark for alerts.

Units of Measure & Conversions

Homogenize your pricing to compare different formats. For example, if you buy Avocado Oil in both 5L jugs and 1L bottles, you can set a Base Unit of Liters:

1. Go to the product's Configuration > Purchases.
2. Select L (Liters) as the Base Unit.
3. Define the conversion (e.g., 1 unit = 5L for the jug). This reveals which format is actually cheaper per liter, optimizing your purchasing strategy.

Grouping Products

Merge identical items from different suppliers (e.g., "Tomato" vs. "Red Tomato") using the "Group with another" tool. This creates a single master product for cleaner reporting.

4. Purchase Reconciliation and Incidences

Reconciliation Logic

Reconciliation links delivery notes to invoices to ensure you only pay for what you received.

* Acceptable Difference: Configure your allowed discrepancy (e.g., €5.00) in User Settings > Configuration > Documents.

Reconciliation Methods

1. Recommended (AI): Our algorithm suggests matches based on supplier, date, and amount.
2. Manual: Select an invoice and its corresponding delivery notes from the Pending Documents list.
3. Advanced: A deep-dive view where you can edit the selection or view specific line items to resolve complex discrepancies.

Incident Tracking

Flag issues (e.g., missing items, damaged goods) during upload or reconciliation by clicking "Add Incident." Track these via status: Created, Under Review, or Resolved.

5. Culinary Engineering: Dynamic Bill of Materials (Scandallos)

Creating and Editing Recipes

In the Scandallos section, you can build recipes that live and breathe with your business:

* Ingredients & Sub-preparations: Add raw products or link other recipes (like a base vinaigrette).
* Dynamic Costing: Dish costs update automatically based on the last purchase price recorded in the system.

Margins and Shrinkage

* Expected Margin Tool: Enter your target margin (e.g., 75%). haddock will provide a Recommended Selling Price (PV). If your current price results in a lower margin, the indicator will turn red as a warning.
* Shrinkage (Merma): Use the formula (Weight of Shrinkage / Total Purchased Weight) * 100. If you aren't sure of the exact percentage, you can use pre-established industry values from standard tables provided within the editor.

Kitchen Documentation

Download a PDF of any recipe for your staff. This export includes the method, ingredients, and quantities, but excludes all financial data to keep your margins private.

6. Business Intelligence: Dashboard and Sales Integration

Dashboard Views

* General View: Based on Invoices; ideal for accounting and a broad business overview.
* Real-time View: Based on Delivery Notes; perfect for monitoring your current margins as goods enter the kitchen.

Sales Data Entry

Import sales to unlock profitability metrics:

1. POS Integration: Connect your system via Settings > My Integrations.
2. Manual Import: Upload an Excel template via the Sales page. Mandatory fields: Date, Subtotal, Tax, and Total.

Article-Scandallo Linking

In the Articles section, look for any items marked with a "Pending" alert. Click Select to link these POS items to your Scandallos.

* The Result: haddock will calculate the "unit benefit" (profitability) of every item sold, helping you identify your "Star Dishes" and eliminate those that are losing you money.

7. Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

This part contains all the knowledge you need to assist users with the Haddock FAQ by the users. It is structured by topic to help you quickly find the answers to customer inquiries.

-----

## 1\. Prices and Plans

**Q: Can I offer a customer a discount?**
**Agent Guidance:** Yes\! Always remind customers that if they subscribe to an annual plan, they will automatically receive a **5% discount**.

**Q: What is the difference between the plans?**
**Agent Guidance:** Explain that the main differences revolve around three factors:

1.  The number of users they can add.
2.  The number of documents they can scan per month.
3.  Whether or not the plan includes the *Product Module* (which is used to control price variations).
    *(Direct them to the pricing page for a detailed breakdown).*

**Q: The customer has reached their document limit. What should they do?**
**Agent Guidance:** \* Advise them to upgrade their plan to Premium (up to 400 documents). Tell them to go to **User Settings \> My Restaurants**, choose their restaurant, and click on **Change plan**.

  * **If they are already on the Premium plan and hit the limit:** Tell them to contact us directly so we can provide a tailor-made enterprise solution.

**Q: How does a customer choose the best plan?**
**Agent Guidance:** Ask them to estimate the volume of invoices and delivery notes they receive and want to digitise monthly, and ask if they need to control price variations using the product module. Match their needs to the respective tier.

-----

## 2\. Payment and Billing

**Q: What are the payment methods?**
**Agent Guidance:** Customers can pay via **credit card**. If they are on an **annual plan**, they also have the option to pay via **direct debit**. (If they want direct debit, instruct them to contact support for the necessary steps).

**Q: How can a customer receive their invoice?**
**Agent Guidance:** Instruct the customer to go to **Update plan**. Under their billing history, they will see an icon allowing them to download the invoice for every payment made.

-----

## 3\. Users and Roles

**Q: Can a customer invite their team? How many?**
**Agent Guidance:** Yes, Haddock features multi-role functionality. Customers can add **up to 7 users**, depending on their specific subscription plan.

**Q: What are the different user roles?**
**Agent Guidance:** There are 3 roles:

1.  **💼 Administrator:** Full access to all functionalities.
2.  **📄 Document Management:** Can add, view, and edit documents and upload sales. *Cannot* access the dashboard or the sales total.
3.  **👀 Manager View:** Can view the dashboard, supplier data, and export documents. *Cannot* edit any information.

**Q: How do customers manage (add, edit, delete) users?**
**Agent Guidance:** Tell them to navigate to **User settings \> My restaurants**. Under "My team", they can add new members. To edit or delete, select the member, click on the 3 dots at the top right, and apply the desired change.

-----

## 4\. Dashboard

**Q: Can all team members view the dashboard?**
**Agent Guidance:** No. Only users with the **Administrator** or **Manager View** role can access the dashboard.

**Q: Can the dashboard display amounts with or without VAT?**
**Agent Guidance:** Yes. The customer can toggle this by going to **User settings \> Settings \> Dashboard** and selecting their preferred option.

**Q: What is the "Real-time view" on the dashboard?**
**Agent Guidance:** \* **Activated:** The dashboard displays the total of delivery notes and tickets, plus the invoices of suppliers that do not provide delivery notes.

  * **Deactivated:** The dashboard displays purely the total of invoices and tickets (a more general, standard overview).

-----

## 5\. Documents Management

### Digitised Documents

**Q: How are documents uploaded?**
**Agent Guidance:** By taking a photo or attaching a file (PDF, JPG, etc.). If they don't have the physical document, they can add the data manually (they will need the base amount, VAT, supplier, date, document type, and number).

**Q: Do manually uploaded expenses count towards the plan's document limit?**
**Agent Guidance:** No. Only documents uploaded via photo or PDF count toward the limit.

**Q: Do deleted documents count towards the monthly limit?**
**Agent Guidance:** Yes. Once a document is digitized, it consumes a credit, even if the user later deletes it.

**Q: How can a customer edit a document with incorrect information?**
**Agent Guidance:** Instruct them to open the document, click the three dots (top right), and select "Edit". They can edit general info (Supplier, type, date) or click the pencil icon next to totals, VAT, and line items.

### Documents Under Review / Failing / Rejected

**Q: Where is a recently uploaded document?**
**Agent Guidance:** It goes to **"Under Review"** while being digitized. If not there, check "Not confirmed" or "Rejected".

**Q: Why does a document "lack confirmation"?**
**Agent Guidance:** The algorithm missed something. Common reasons:

  * **Poor image quality:** Tell the user to delete and re-upload.
  * **Lack of information:** Missing date, amounts, etc. Tell the user to get a complete document from the supplier.
  * **Supplier not detected:** The VAT number wasn't found. The user just needs to manually select the supplier.

**Q: Why would a document be "Rejected"?**
**Agent Guidance:** \* **Mixed documents:** Multiple invoices in one photo. (Must be uploaded separately).

  * **Duplicate:** Already digitized.
  * **Upload error:** Image failed to display.

-----

## 6\. Reconciliation

**Q: What is reconciliation?**
**Agent Guidance:** It links delivery notes to the final invoice. It ensures the restaurant actually received all the goods they are being billed for.

**Q: How does a user reconcile documents?**
**Agent Guidance:** There are 3 ways:

1.  **Recommended reconciliation:** Our algorithm suggests links based on supplier, dates, and amounts within the "acceptable difference".
2.  **Manual reconciliation:** The user manually selects the invoice and corresponding delivery notes.
3.  **Advanced options:** For editing selections or viewing finer details.

**Q: What is the "Acceptable difference"?**
**Agent Guidance:** The allowed monetary discrepancy between the sum of the delivery notes and the invoice total. Users can configure this in **User settings \> Configuration \> Documents**.

**Q: A supplier only gives invoices, no delivery notes. How to stop reconciliation prompts?**
**Agent Guidance:** Go to **Suppliers**, select the specific supplier, and *deactivate* the option: **"Deliver with delivery notes or I have an invoice for the tickets"**.

-----

## 7\. Downloads & Exports

**Q: How are documents exported and in what formats?**
**Agent Guidance:** Users can select files in **Documents** and click **Export**. They can choose between **Excel, PDF, or CSV**.

**Q: What data breakdowns can be exported?**
**Agent Guidance:** They can choose:

1.  Summaries and totals (VAT in rows)
2.  By products (all line items exported)
3.  With VAT in columns.

-----

## 8\. Suppliers

**Q: A freelance supplier isn't found by the scanner. What to do?**
**Agent Guidance:** Create them manually in **Suppliers \> New supplier**. It is crucial to put the freelance ID number in the **VAT number field** so the system recognizes it on future uploads.

**Q: What supplier details CANNOT be edited after creation?**
**Agent Guidance:** NIF/CIF (VAT number), the fiscal name, and the address cannot be edited later. Commercial name, code, and category *can* be edited.

**Q: Can a user bulk-change the category of a supplier's documents?**
**Agent Guidance:** Yes. In the Suppliers module, edit the supplier. Change the category, save, and then edit the provider again and activate the option to "change all documents to the category already selected".

-----

## 9\. Incidences

**Q: How and when are incidents created?**
**Agent Guidance:** Users can log incidents during document upload or reconciliation by clicking **Add incident**, selecting the reason, and setting a severity level.

**Q: What are the incident statuses?**
**Agent Guidance:** Created, Under Review, and Resolved. Resolved ones are found in **Incidents \> Resolved incidents**.

-----

## 10\. Staff Costs

**Q: How are staff costs added?**
**Agent Guidance:** Manually via **Expenses \> Personal \> Add**. Currently, users *cannot* upload staff costs via automated photo scanning, but they can attach payslips/documents manually. These expenses *will* reflect on the dashboard.

-----

## 11\. Products Module

**Q: How does the system extract products?**
**Agent Guidance:** Haddock extracts line items from invoices/delivery notes under the categories of raw materials, beverages, and maintenance/cleaning. It matches them if the name and unit of measure are almost exact (Note: Case consistency matters\! "Lettuce" and "LECHUGA" won't match automatically if spelled wildly differently, but exact case matches work).

**Q: What happens if a product is scanned incorrectly?**
**Agent Guidance:** The user should access the product, click the icon at the end of the line to view the original document, and manually edit the description so it matches their other purchases.

**Q: How do users handle different units (Box/Kg/Units) or merge suppliers?**
**Agent Guidance:** \* **Merging:** Use **Group with another**, select the products, give it a unified name, and set base units.

  * **Conversions:** Go to **Configuration \> Purchases** on the product, set a base unit (Kg, liters, units), and add the conversion data (e.g., 1 Box = 5 Kg).

**Q: What is a "Reference Price"?**
**Agent Guidance:** It tells the user if they are overpaying. It can be based on the historical average unit price of all purchases, or a manual tariff price set by the user.

-----

## 12\. Bill of Materials (Scandallos)

**Q: What is a Bill of Materials / Cost Estimate?**
**Agent Guidance:** It is a breakdown of all the ingredients, quantities, and costs required to make a specific dish in the restaurant, allowing the user to calculate their profit margin.

**Q: What is "shrinkage" (Merma)?**
**Agent Guidance:** Shrinkage is the loss of product during preparation (e.g., potato skins, fish bones, thawing weight loss).
*Calculation:* (Weight of shrinkage / Total purchased weight) \* 100 = Shrinkage percentage.

**Q: Can users import products if they don't have an invoice yet?**
**Agent Guidance:** Yes, products can be imported directly via Excel to be used in the Bill of Materials immediately.
